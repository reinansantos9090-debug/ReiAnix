# ReiAnix monetization preview

- Five configured production AdMob blocks, but the default APK uses Google's sample IDs only.
- Preview: optional rewarded ads, 24h suppression, local per-item art unlocks, simulated Premium and reset in the Premium dialog. No real payment. Three free banners and wallpapers; first four avatars, default avatar and first three frames are free. Already selected avatars/frames are retained.
- Real purchase: create a non-consumable one-time product `reianix_premium` in Play Console, set Brazil base price to R$ 9.90, activate the purchase option, obtain the RSA billing public key, use license testers and an internal/closed Play track. Price displayed from Play, never hardcoded for purchase. Build with `-PreianixEnablePlayBilling=true -PreianixBillingPublicKey=<public key>` and omit live ads for purchase testing. Preview's simulated state is isolated from release entitlements. Do not consume the product.
- Live ads are deliberately disabled unless the release also opts in with `-PreianixEnableLiveAds=true`. Before doing that, check all five AdMob IDs, configure UMP privacy messages, update the public privacy policy/terms and Play Data Safety, complete AdMob review and app-ads.txt where required, and confirm all distribution/monetization rights. Do not click live ads or use them for tests.
- Real purchases are verified using Google's signed response locally and acknowledged; query purchases on foreground to recover/refund state. Client-only verification is not tamper-resistant; a secure backend is recommended before public monetization. Reward unlocks are local; they are not synchronized by Play Billing.
- No advertisements are placed in the player. Free users retain essential playback, accessibility-related subtitle size/position, offline library and metadata. Premium locks advanced subtitle colors/fonts and shortcut layout.
- Build/CI and on-device checks still required. Test reward cancellation, no fill, offline, process restart, expiry, profile art, refund/restore, and playback.

- The DAY reward now grants temporary Premium for 24 hours; it does not create a Play purchase. The Home offer lets users renew after expiry. Individually rewarded art unlocks remain local and separate.
